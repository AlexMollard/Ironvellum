Why choose Ironvellum over Hevy?

Choose Ironvellum if you want to build your own program, track weighted and bodyweight lifts together, and keep the training log on your phone.

Get Ironvellum on F-Droid
Muscle coverage without a Pro plan
Hevy puts set counts per muscle group and its warm-up calculator in Hevy Pro. Ironvellum includes both for free. Its planner also shows where you’re below your weekly target and suggests exercises to cover the gap.
Separate records for different lifts
A pull-up, an assisted pull-up and an archer pull-up each keep their own progression. You can change variations without overwriting the mark you’re chasing.
A log you can keep offline
Train and export your history without signing in. If you want a web dashboard, turn on sync or run your own backend. Progress photos stay on your phone.
