Ironvellum is a free, offline strength tracker. It plans your training, makes logging fast, and shows you what’s working.

PLAN
• Answer a few questions and get a weekly plan for your goal and the gear you own, from a full gym to a pull-up bar.
• See which muscles your week covers, and fill any gap in a tap.

TRAIN
• Log a set with one tap; last session’s numbers carry over.
• Rest timers, warm-ups, timed holds and notes built in.
• It tells you when to add weight.

GROW
• Hundreds of exercises with drawings, and bodyweight skills on progression paths.
• Earn XP, levels and deeds as you train.
• Charts for your lifts and bodyweight, with optional Health Connect.

YOURS
• Works fully offline. No ads, no trackers, no paid tier.
• Optional account to back up and train with friends.
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
