# Review — 2026-09-30 approachability pass

Things found while walking the app on the emulator that are worth doing but
were out of scope for the approachability work, or need the owner's call.
Fixed items are listed at the bottom so the review reads as a record.

## Fixed in this pass

- Catalogue: Lying Leg Raise and Dead Bug added as tier I floor ab movements, so a first-year cycle with no kit trains abs again.
- Glossary-wide vocabulary, one name per concept (`docs/GLOSSARY.md`).
- Onboarding: visible skip consequence; plain field label and footer.
- Today: add-height-and-weight prompt with a direct button; Open Trial when there is no cycle; rank/ascension explainer.
- "What's this?" explainers for BMI, FFMI, Navy tape, double progression, deload.
- Settings: name button says Save; unset armoury no longer shows "Nothing"; BACK label centred.
- Respite days offer "Begin <next rite> now" until a trial is sealed that day; the rest-day art shrinks so the button stays on screen.
- Live trial: an unset load on a barbell lift reads "kg", not "BW"; column labels 8sp → 10sp; the notes panel is "NAME & NOTES" (Chronicle is the history); the seal prompt says unticked sets won't count; the sealed card says "DEED EARNED".
- Rite-update prompt after a short trial reads plainly.
- Trial detail: unset weighted sets read "5 reps", not "5×BW".
- `inkBorder` with a zero width drew a hairline: every training-calendar date wore today's gold ring. Fixed once in the shared modifier.
- Deed progress "2 day oath to go" → "2 days to go"; strength deeds spell out "estimated 1-rep max".
- Journal and Daily empty states say what fills them; Health Connect caption lists everything it reads.
- Forge: load reasons use body text and carry the 1-rep max explainer.
- Onboarding skip line points to Settings for the body and Rites for the cycle.
- Sentence captions moved from labelSmall to bodySmall (35 sites); labelSmall itself is 11sp at 0.5sp tracking.
- Forge with no rites: one "Take this cycle" button, no "Replace my cycle".
- Rites: the empty state offers the Forge; the New Rite chooser explains each option.
- Codex: subtitle counts deeds and techniques in words; deed bars wrap to two lines; the level deeds sit under "Level".
- Account: says up front that an account is optional and what it adds.
- A zero strength score is hidden on the Chronicle row and trial detail, not shown as a gold "0".
- `tools/device.py` prints UTF-8, so `labels` no longer crashes on glyphs.
- Forge: a first-year (Low volume) cycle never prescribes a bodyweight progression past tier III; Dragon Flags were landing in beginner no-kit cycles.
- `docs/ART_ATTRIBUTION.md` no longer lists the `ic_rank_*` emblems or the "Rank emblem set" section; the drawables are gone from `res`.
- `tools/gate.py` calls `gradlew.bat` by absolute path and drops `NoDefaultCurrentDirectoryInExePath` from the child env; green under `NoDefaultCurrentDirectoryInExePath=1`.
- `tools/device.py install` prints the APK's build time and age, and warns when `app/src/main` has newer files.
- Split picker: the caption for "Upper / lower + PPL" says PPL is push / pull / legs.
- Forge shortfall note: "add a day" only when time was the limit and fewer than 6 days; otherwise it points at priorities, and the kit-short case says a fuller armoury would cover them.
- Onboarding: the armoury has no default. The lifter picks (or picks Nothing for bodyweight) before the training step continues, with a caption saying why; skipping onboarding leaves it unset. The Forge starts from the saved armoury and, when unset, shows no preview until one is picked, instead of assuming Full gym.
